# European elections manifesto audit — 2026-06-16

Compared top-level party manifesto files against the UK-wide result tables on the
Wikipedia pages for the 1979–2019 UK European Parliament elections.

## Added in this pass (PDFs)
2004 Independent (Gilliland); 2009 Alliance, Conservative, DUP, Labour; 2014 Alliance,
Conservative NI, DUP, Liberal Democrats, SNP; 2019 DUP. Empty 1984 and 1989 year
folders created.

## Still missing (high level)
- **1979** — only Conservative images; missing Labour, Liberal, SNP, DUP, SDLP, UUP,
  Plaid, Alliance, Ecology, Mebyon Kernow, etc.
- **1984, 1989** — no manifestos held at all.
- **1994** — only Conservative images present.
- **1999** — only the PES group manifesto at top level; most UK party manifestos missing.
- **2004** — missing UKIP, Lib Dems, BNP, Respect, Plaid, English Democrat and others.
- **2009** — still missing UKIP, Plaid, Socialist Labour, No2EU, Libertas, etc.
- **2014** — still missing Sinn Féin, An Independence from Europe, BNP, English
  Democrats, NHA, and minor NI entries.
- **2019** — Wikipedia-linked manifestos now held (Alliance, Change UK, DUP, GPEW,
  Labour, Lib Dems, Plaid, Sinn Féin, Scottish Greens, UKIP). Not found/unpublished:
  Brexit Party, Conservatives (did not publish), SDLP, TUV, Yorkshire Party, others.

## Notes
- 2019: Conservatives did not publish a manifesto or hold a launch; Engender's summary
  records "No manifesto published" for the Brexit Party and Scottish Conservatives.
- Pre-2004 manifestos are widely cited but rarely available as downloadable PDFs.
- Name variants to watch: Sinn Féin/Sinn Fein, Green/GPEW, UUP/Ulster Unionist, NI21.

## Sources
Wikipedia result pages; CAIN manifesto index and 2009/2014 NI pages; UK Parliament
briefing SN06895 (2014 URLs); Internet Archive captures; Engender 2019 EU summary.
