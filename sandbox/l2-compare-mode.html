L2 — Compare mode (MVP)

Example surface: the Documents grid on /election/2024. Select 2–3 parties that have online text, then open a simple side-by-side reading view. No automatic policy alignment or AI summarising.

What this mock proposes
  • Checkboxes on manifesto cards that have a readable text edition.
  • PDF-only / no-text cards stay visible but cannot be selected.
  • Sticky bar when 2–3 selected → Compare.
  • Compare view: one column per party (stacks on narrow screens), excerpt + link to full reader.
