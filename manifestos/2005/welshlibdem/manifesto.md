## Putting Patients First

It is not asking much, surely? A National Health Service that helps you stay healthy, and makes sure that when you do fall ill you are treated quickly, irrespective of wealth or status.

Yet despite the investment now going in - which Liberal Democrats so famously pressed the government to deliver

Mark Williams Ceredigion

The urgent problems in the NHS, such as long waiting times, need urgent and emergency action. The Welsh Liberal Democrats will put patients first and publish a full action plan of measures. Some of the highlights of the immediate measures we would introduce include:

### Establishing Walk-in Health Centres

Establish Walk-In Health Centres across Wales. These would be open seven days a week and offer fast and convenient access to local treatment and NHS services, providing treatment for patients who might otherwise go to Accident & Emergency departments. Treatments available would include dressings, blood tests and minor ailment treatments. They would be linked to the out-of-hours services and operate ambulance triaging service, so the ambulances themselves can take patients to the most appropriate treatment facility.

### Providing Quicker Diagnosis, Better Use of Diagnostic Equipment

Your chances of surviving life threatening and debilitating illnesses are higher with swift diagnosis. When a GP believes you may have a serious illness, we will offer diagnosis by the quickest practical route, whether public or private, so that the NHS can treat you more quickly. More tests and scans will be available in places like GPs' surgeries and pharmacies. We will introduce elective surgery schedules that run on a 24-hour cycle, when staffing levels allow.

### Making Better Use of Community Hospitals

We would provide more community-based intermediate care facilities across Wales, reducing the risk of discharge from hospital prematurely and consequent emergency re-admission. This would offer a better route through the different stages of treatment and allow patients to receive the best care.
