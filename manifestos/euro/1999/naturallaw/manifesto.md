### UNITY AND SOVEREIGNTY

### Creating a European Union of truly sovereign nations – the first step to a peaceful united world

Unity and harmony cannot be achieved in a state of dependence. ## Sovereignty instead of subsidiarity

### SOVEREIGNTY INSTEAD OF SUBSIDIARITY

### The principle of sovereignty should take precedence over the principle of subsidiarity

 The Natural Law Party believes that the principle of subsidiarity in the EU should be replaced by the principle of putting first the sovereignty, independence and self-sufficiency of every member state.
 If the collective consciousness of Europe is integrated, and the national consciousness of each nation is strong and self-sufficient, then new policies will naturally be in favour of all nations and the Union as a whole.

 A harmonious and well-functioning European Union will only become a living reality if none of its sovereign member states are forced to accept a treaty or directive from the Union at any time, and if every decision that can be taken at the national level is taken at the national level.

 For any European treaty each country should have the freedom not to participate, or to do so at a later date (as, for example, has already happened with Economic and Monetary Union or the Schengen
 Treaty). No country should be forced by the European Union to adopt a policy that it does not like. It will also be necessary to develop a satisfactory mechanism for member states to modify their participation in a treaty they have already joined, in case the conditions of the treaty are no longer acceptable to the nation.

COMMON DEFENCE POLICY

### Creating invincible defence by maintaining a coherence-creating group in Europe and on every continent

 Dr Nigel Kahn

 D EFENCES POKESMAN
 Security and invincible defence will never be established by amassing arms, but only by creating lasting and stable peace in the world.

 The only true security for Europe, both internal and external, lies in securing the real friendship of all other nations, not in posing a bigger threat with a large and well-armed military.

 The Natural Law Party therefore supports the idea of a common defence policy for the European Union, but not the maintenance of a common military force. On the basis of its prevention-oriented approach to defence,
 the Natural Law Party proposes that the EU should maintain a coherence-creating group of 7000 experts in Maharishi’s Transcendental Meditation and Yogic Flying in Europe, and a similar group on each continent. The extensive scientific research on these programme shows that this will raise integrity, stability and harmony in world consciousness to such a degree that regional or international conflicts will simply not arise.

 In this situation, there will be no need for a European military task force for use in the context of conflicts within the EU, between the
 EU and other regions, or in any part of the world. The coherence-creating groups maintained by the EU on all continents will be a true ‘peace-keeping’
 force, securing the immunity of Europe and all parts of the world from hostility and conflict, and maintaining harmony among all nations.

 This is the only intelligent basis for a sound common defence policy.
 The Natural Law Party’s unique programme to create and maintain world peace renders obsolete the present EU debate on the framework for military alliance, including the involvement of Nato, the WEU, or the so-called "partnership for peace".

### The war in Yugoslavia

 The war in the Balkans emphasises the need for a fundamentally new and effective approach to solve international crises. This approach displays a mode of foreign policy that says: "If you do not conform to my idea of internal and foreign policy, I will bomb you."

 Military action not only reveals a distinct lack of intelligence, it poses an enormous danger to the world; violence always begets more violence.

 The military powers involved in the Nato attacks on Yugoslavia can expect not only that more violence will erupt in the Balkans, but also that the balance of power in the world will become dangerously destabilised.
 There is an eternal principle "As you sow, so shall you reap."
 Therefore, an upsurge of acts of violence and negativity must also be anticipated in the Nato nations themselves.

 There is no doubt that oppression and cruelty has been prevalent in
 Yugoslavia; however, further acts of violence will only serve to worsen the situation and pose the greatest threat to peace and international security seen for many decades.

 The scientifically validated programme to create peace based on establishing a coherence-creating group of experts in Transcendental Meditation and
 Yogic Flying is no longer a luxury, or something that can be evaluated at leisure. How long can anyone stand idly by, knowing that an effective and reliable solution is available when there is the danger that the fire of war will burn more savagely and spread further in Europe and in other parts of the world? The Natural Law Party will – whether it is elected into office or not – place as an urgent priority the creation of permanent groups of coherence-creating experts in Yogic
 Flying. The immediate need is for a group of 2000 experts, to neutralise the stress in the Balkans region; the next priority is to establish a permanent group of 7000 to guarantee the future peace of the world.

## Economic and monetary union

### ECONOMIC AND MONETARY UNION

### Securing a stable foundation for Economic and Monetary Union

 Anthony Hardy

 SPOKESMAN FORE CONOMY
 For several years the Natural Law Party has maintained that under the wrong conditions Economic and Monetary Union (EMU) could have disastrous consequences for the economies of all member states and the EU as a whole. It is highly dangerous to impose EMU when the EU is undoubtedly not ready for it and it could easily jeopardise the future of the EU itself.

 This is not only an economic issue but also an issue of sovereignty since the Euro will quickly undermine the sovereignty of member states. With incoherent European collective consciousness, EMU could easily create serious new tensions in Europe as the advantages and disadvantages become clear for different member states, and the people of disadvantaged regions begin to realise that it is an economic regime from which there is no easy escape. However, monetary union clearly also has advantages in that it provides stability for internal and international trade, it disallows predatory speculation on a national currency, and it disallows one nation from artificially manipulating its currency for unfair trade advantage.

 The Natural Law Party therefore believes that the two most important requirements for EMU are: to create stable conditions under which it can work effectively, and to protect the sovereignty of each member state. To be effective, monetary union must be based on true stability of the economy of each member state. Economic stability cannot, however, be artificially contrived as is clear from the turbulent economic history of each EU country during recent decades. Economic stability and dynamism can be created only by strengthening the real basis of the economy, which means unfolding the full creative potential of the population, and creating coherence, integration and dynamism in the collective consciousness.

 Now that a single currency has been implemented for eleven member states since 1 January 1999, it is very urgent to use the best means to bring stability to the new currency, the Euro. Since the coherence of collective consciousness is the real basis of the stability of the economy throughout Europe, the Natural Law Party strongly advocates the immediate establishment of a coherence-creating group in Europe.

 Only with a stable collective consciousness, and through that a stable economy, will the Euro have a chance of becoming a stable currency.

 On this basis, the Natural Law Party believes that the current purely economic criteria for EMU are quite inadequate and proposes an additional stability criterion: the stability of collective consciousness throughout the EU. This would apply both to countries already committed to joining and those who may wish to do so in the future. The Natural Law Party also recommends that the central banks of the eleven Euro countries should be maintained, and that the European Central Bank (ECB) should make its decisions in conjunction with them.

 The Natural Law Party's policy is that EU member states should continue to be completely free to decide for themselves when and whether they wish to participate in EMU. The implementation of Natural Law programmes will not only create a stable foundation for EMU, but will also create the basis for full employment, as confirmed by extensive scientific research.

 Regarding the decision pending in the UK about joining the single currency, the Natural Law Party's policy is first to apply nationally the new stability criterion of creating a coherent, stable collective consciousness, the basis of a stable, progressive, problem-free economy. While other parties discuss a timetable for monetary union on the basis of purely economic and political factors, we believe that a decision should be made only after the Natural Law Party's new stability criteria have been met. Only when there will be no risk of losing out should the nation decide to join.

 The next step will be to make a democratic decision through national referendum. We suggest three options for the referendum:

 To join the single currency, which will mean abandoning the pound and using only the Euro To stay out of monetary union altogether and keep the pound

- To use both the pound and the Euro, with the two linked by an exchange rate mechanism set by the UK government.

 With the third option, the UK will be able to keep the pound as a symbol of national identity and maintain a degree of control over the national economy, while having the advantages of also using the Euro.

## Automation in Administration

### AUTOMATION IN ADMINISTRATION

### Increasing efficiency and reducing bureaucracy in the EU

 The theme of Natural Law government can be summarised in the expression "Automation in Administration". The Government of Nature operates with supreme efficiency, using what is known in physics as the "principle of least action".

 Nature's intelligence spontaneously computes the path of minimum effort for any action.

 Through this principle of supreme economy, the whole of the universe is silently and efficiently governed. The Natural
 Law Party applies this principle in designing its approach to administration and setting policy in each area. Effective government is streamlined government; it absorbs as little as possible of the taxpayers’
 money in conducting its tasks silently and efficiently. Nowhere is this principle more needed than in the affairs of the European Union.
 Precious financial resources from each country are needlessly spent in simply maintaining the over-manned, over-complex, and inefficient bureaucracy of the various components of the European administrative machinery.

 Not only is an overly bureaucratic administrative system expensive; it is actually less able to fulfil its mandate to serve the people than a slimmer administration. There is so much inertia within the system as it administers itself that little energy is available for its external functions. The extent of inertia has recently been illustrated by the inability of the Commission to respond to the auditors’
 report on the EU accounts showing widespread fraud and misuse of funds resulting in the resignation of the Commission as a whole.

 The Natural
 Law Party would therefore take all necessary measures as soon as possible to make the administration of the European Union efficient and proficient in serving the peoples of Europe.

 The Natural Law Party is particularly well placed to achieve this objective since its scientifically proven policies will reduce and eventually eliminate the problems that occupy so much time and attention of the EU administration – failure and problems in all areas of the economy, agriculture, law enforcement, immigration etc.

## Natural Agriculture

### Making agriculture natural, healthy and in favour of the farmer, the consumer and the environment

 Mark Griffiths E NVIRONMENTS POKESMAN

 Dr James Brewster

 A GRICULTURES POKESMAN

 The hazards associated with the transformation from traditional farming to industrialised, agrochemical-dependent farming have clearly been exacerbated by the economic framework set by EU Agricultural Policy. The artificial constraints set by EU Agricultural Policy have often resulted in even higher usage of chemicals, as well as over-production and waste, and a loss of the traditional diversity of crop production. The Natural Law Party will implement an urgent and complete reform of EU Agricultural Policy to rectify these imbalances – reforms that will be far more substantial and address the problems on a much deeper level than anything being undertaken in the EU at the moment. The aim will be to encourage sustainable systems of farming, minimise bureaucratic intervention to farmers and promote organic agriculture, fully in alliance with Natural Law, while restoring the responsibility, self-sufficiency, and prestige of farmers.

 To achieve these objectives the Natural Law Party's policy will be to phase out EU agricultural subsidies over a period of seven years, at the same time as implementing a new approach that promotes a transformation of agriculture in Europe to natural, sustainable systems of agriculture, particularly organic systems. To facilitate this, agriculture subsidies will be phased out first for conventional agriculture and some of the funds transferred to research projects to develop natural sustainable systems of agriculture and to make them more productive.

 There will be regular reviews of the success of this important transformation of European agriculture in the direction of creating natural, healthy food for everyone. While the goal is to phase out subsidies, attention will be given to areas in special need in order to ensure that they still receive essential support either on a European or national level.

 An important aspect of the transformation will be to use a part of the savings for special education and training for farmers and the general public. Farmers will be introduced gradually to the importance of Vedic farming – Natural Law-based farming – and to programmes that will bring the consciousness of the farmer in accord with the nourishing power of Natural Law. The Natural Law Party's educational programmes will restore the dignity of farmers in the eyes of the public and raise the awareness of everyone to the importance of natural food for every aspect of health and the environment. This will include alerting everyone to the fact that fresh and locally grown food (not transported over long distances) has profound health benefits for the consumer and is an important way to protect the environment. The high standard and quality of such future agricultural production in Europe must necessarily be protected against imports of products of inferior quality from outside Europe.

 As agricultural subsides are reduced some of the funds will also be transferred to integrated rural development programmes that will strengthen the economic and social vitality of the EU’s rural areas while restoring and protecting the natural environment. A complete ban on genetically modified foods

 The Natural Law Party will place an immediate and total EU-wide ban on all genetically modified agricultural products, including field trials, commercial production, and imports

 The party believes that there is no alternative to a ban because of the serious, scientifically established hazards of GMOS for human health, biodiversity and the environment. The emphasis for progress in agriculture should instead be on the readily available natural alternatives to provide healthy food for the whole population.

 Public debate about genetically modified (GM) food has taken centre stage in many European countries this year, including Britain. With continuing extensive media coverage of the issues surrounding gm foods, people are better informed about the risks of gm foods, and opinion polls show clearly that the great majority do not want them. The Natural Law Party can take much credit for this situation since it was the first political party to take up this issue and has been actively campaigning world-wide for a ban on GM foods for several years.

### Natural Law programmes are the only basis for successful EU enlargement

 In the acceptance of new member states there must be two prerequisites. One is that the European Union is harmonious, prosperous and integrated within itself, and the other is that the new member has risen to an adequate level with a coherent, integrated national consciousness and the full support of Natural Law.

 A strong, harmonious whole cannot be built by putting together incoherent parts.

 Therefore the Natural Law Party proposes that Natural Law programmes should be implemented not only within each member state but also in applicant states. These scientifically validated programmes will enliven the support of Natural Law for each country, bringing economic growth and stability, strengthened national integrity, reduced crime, and improved health, education and national security. They will support national sovereignty and self-sufficiency at the same time as supporting harmonious relations between nations on matters of common interest. Only on the basis of developing these qualities can new countries join the EU without any danger of weakening the integrity and economic stability of the existing EU member states.

The index will include a wide variety of measures, such as crime rates, sickness levels, pollution, economic stability and growth, the simplicity of administration, reduction of taxes, naturalness of agriculture, and signs of growing cultural integrity and improvements to the environment.

 Europe is a beautiful mosaic of cultures, languages, races and values. If the national consciousness of each country is brought into alliance with Natural Law through the programmes of the Natural Law Party, then integration will proceed naturally and without struggle, while sovereignty is fully maintained.

## Democracy in the EU

### DEMOCRACY IN THE EU

### Natural Law Party calls for an end to undemocratic election procedures

 The Natural Law Party believes that election procedures should be as open and democratic as possible in order to bring in fresh, new ideas to create a better government. At present, in some EU countries there are very restrictive electoral requirements which in effect prevent new parties from entering the elections (for example, the requirement for very large numbers of signatures in Italy, Germany and Denmark). These restrictions serve the interests of the old parties, but keep valuable new ideas out of the election debate. The EU Natural Law Parties will therefore work in a concerted way to bring an end to restrictive and undemocratic election procedures, both for national and European elections in EU member states. Some basic requirements are obviously necessary, but for true democracy these should be minimal in every country.

 In general, the Natural Law Party would like to see voting systems of absolute proportional representation so that every group in society and every party has a voice in government.

 This will truly encourage the introduction of new knowledge and new solutions, rather than supporting just the failed approaches of the past. The administration of Natural Law will bring satisfaction to every citizen and not just to the majority.
