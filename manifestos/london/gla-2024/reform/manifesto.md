# Reform UK London Mayoral Manifesto 2024

## London needs Reform:

*   **SCRAP ALL OF ULEZ**
    I will scrap all ULEZ charges throughout the whole of London.
*   **REFUND ULEZ FEES & FINES**
    I will refund all expansion fees and fines received since August 29th 2023.
*   **REMOVE LTNs & 20MPH ZONES**
    I will remove needless road restrictions, pinch points, floating bus stops and excessively wide cycle lanes.
*   **TRIPLE BOBBIES ON THE BEAT**
    I will make London's streets, public transport and especially the Underground safer.
*   **TRIPLE AFFORDABLE HOUSING**
    I will triple affordable housing builds, particularly on GLA and TfL owned land.
*   **PROTECT LONDON'S HERITAGE**
    I will commission a statue of Queen Elizabeth II to be placed in Hyde Park.
