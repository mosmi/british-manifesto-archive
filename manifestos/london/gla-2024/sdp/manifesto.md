### For a Safe and Beautiful City

## Foreword
FOR MAYOR OF LONDON

On Thursday 2nd May you have a choice. You can vote for more of the same – rising crime, ugly building, division and decay – or you can vote to make London safe, beautiful and united once again.

As a psychiatric nurse, I see first-hand the failure of our city. I see it in the drug use and antisocial behaviour on our streets, the rise in knife crime, and the breakdown of families.

The current Mayor is focused on identity politics. I will focus on what matters. I want to build a London where our streets are safe to walk on, our public services work, and our buildings inspire us.

**SAFE**
London should be a city where people feel safe. I will double the number of police on our streets, and focus resources on tackling violent crime and antisocial behaviour.

**BEAUTIFUL**
London is a beautiful city, but it is being ruined by ugly, low-quality developments. I will establish a design code to ensure new buildings respect London's heritage.

**UNITED**
We need to build a sense of community and shared belonging. I will end the politics of division and focus on what unites us as Londoners.

#### Policies for London

*   **Safe Streets:** Put more police on the beat and focus on community safety.
*   **Housing:** Promote beautiful, high-quality, and traditional architecture.
*   **Transport:** Maintain and improve our transport network, keeping fares affordable.
*   **Heritage:** Protect London's historic buildings and green spaces.

Vote for a safe, beautiful, and united London.

**Vote SDP.**
